The Property

Designed for quiet, built for connection.

Casa Kaiman is a small, owner-run development on the inland edge of Guiones — close enough to walk to the wave with your board still wet, far enough to sleep through anything but the howler monkeys at sunrise.

Each villa was built to disappear into the canopy: open walls, deep terraces, private pools cut into the jungle floor. The apartments are smaller and more intimate, made for couples, surfers and one-night-then-three-more types.

Why Nosara

A Blue Zone surf town where things still move slowly.

Nosara is one of only five Blue Zones in the world — places where people live measurably longer, calmer, more connected lives. From the front door of Casa Kaiman you can walk to Playa Guiones in under three minutes, paddle out, then come back for fresh fruit and coffee.

We live here too. Whatever you want — surf lessons, a yoga shala, a horseback ride at sunset, the best ceviche in town — just ask.
